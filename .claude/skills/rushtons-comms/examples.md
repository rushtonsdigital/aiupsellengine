## Client-provided examples (2026-07-08)

**Cross-sell sample box — introducing a missing category (dairy):**
> Hi team, we noticed you haven't tried our dairy range yet. We'd love to send you a
> complimentary sample box this week - think Clarence Court eggs, Rodda's clotted cream
> and a couple of other things we think you'll love. No strings, just wanted you to see
> what we have. Shall we put one together for you?

**Cross-sell sample box — bar and room service:**
> Hi team, we don't think you've tried our bar range yet. We'd love to send over a
> complimentary box - fresh citrus, specialist juices and a few things that work
> brilliantly behind the bar. Happy to drop it with your next delivery. Want us to put
> one together?

**Cross-sell sample box — dry stores:**
> Hi team, we've got a brilliant dry stores range that we think would work really well
> alongside what you're already ordering from us. We'd love to send you a complimentary
> taster box - oils, vinegars, some Callebaut chocolate and a few other things worth
> knowing about. Shall we include it with your next delivery?

**Lapsed customer re-engagement — sample box:**
> Hi team, it's been a little while and we've missed you! We'd love to tempt you back
> with a complimentary box of what's looking best on the floor this week - some
> brilliant seasonal British produce and a couple of things we think your kitchen will
> love. No obligation, just wanted to say hello. Shall we put one together?

**Post sample box follow-up:**
> Hi team, just wanted to check the box arrived safely and see what you thought.
> Anything in there you'd like to add to your next order? Happy to chat through the
> range if it's useful.
